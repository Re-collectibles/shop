Privacy & search settings

We use search trends to decide which books and other items to add to Re-collectibles. Search analytics start automatically. You can turn them off below and continue browsing and searching.

What we collect

We use Google Analytics to record the words you search for, how many listings match, and whether you searched the full catalogue or new listings. Searches are recorded after a short pause or when you press Enter. Please keep personal information out of the search box.

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish browsers and sessions and processes technical information such as browser and device details. We disable advertising signals in our integration. These reports are available to the shop owner and people they authorise, and are not published in our GitHub repository.

Obvious email addresses and web addresses are excluded from the search events. This does not detect every kind of personal information. Searches longer than 100 characters and single-character searches are also excluded.

Google processes analytics data on its servers, which may be outside New Zealand. Read Google’s privacy policy and Google Analytics privacy information.

Your choice

Search analytics start automatically unless you turn them off below.

We save your choice in your browser’s local storage, including any previous choice to turn analytics off. You can change it here at any time. Turning analytics off stops future search events and removes this shop’s analytics cookies; it does not delete data already sent to Google. If you clear your browser’s storage, analytics will start automatically again. If your browser blocks local storage, your choice lasts only while this page remains open.

Website hosting and enquiries

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and the catalogue pages also load fonts and other resources from external services. Those services may receive connection information such as your IP address. See the GitHub privacy statement.
